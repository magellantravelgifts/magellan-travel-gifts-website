Privacy Policy

Magellan Travel Gifts privacy policy.

This policy explains how Magellan Travel Gifts handles information related to this website, owned social media publishing, and product marketing workflows.

Last updated

May 23, 2026

Magellan Travel Gifts does not sell personal information. This website is a public product and brand information site that links to our Etsy shop and owned social media profiles.

We may use standard website hosting, analytics, ecommerce, and social media tools to understand site performance, maintain our pages, and publish content about our own products. These services may process technical information such as browser type, device type, referring pages, approximate location, IP address, and interaction data according to their own policies.

Our Pinterest publishing workflow is used only to create and publish content to Magellan Travel Gifts owned Pinterest accounts from our own Etsy product listings, images, descriptions, and links. It is not used to access, sell, or share private Pinterest user data.

If you contact us directly, we may use the information you provide to respond to your message, support your request, or maintain ordinary business records.

For privacy questions, contact Magellan Travel Gifts through the contact options available on our Etsy shop: etsy.com/shop/MagellanTravelGifts.
